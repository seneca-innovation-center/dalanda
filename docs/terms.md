## What Dalanda is

Dalanda is a community project to build a language model for Tunisian Arabic (Derja). It is a research project, still in early development, and no Dalanda model is trained yet. On this site you can try a Derja chat demo and propose datasets by sharing links to them. Maintainers review each link against the project's roadmap and its licence and provenance rules.

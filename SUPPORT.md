# Getting help

- **How to contribute:** read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [README](README.md).
- **A question or a problem with this repository:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/seneca-innovation-center/dalanda/issues/new/choose).
- **Removing a source for rights or privacy reasons:** follow the [data policy](docs/data-policy.md#ask-for-a-link-to-be-removed).
- **A security problem:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
- **Anything else:** email [admin@seneca.center](mailto:admin@seneca.center).
